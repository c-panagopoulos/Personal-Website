# About Charalampos Panagopoulos

I'm a full-stack developer based in Athens, Greece. Before writing my first line of production code I
spent years on customer support lines, which taught me that most debugging is just listening carefully to
what someone is actually experiencing.

I transitioned into software development after studying Theology, drawn in by the same instinct that made
me good at support: understanding a system well enough to explain it simply. I'm self-taught in web
development, with a particular interest in self-hosting, home automation, and building small AI-powered
tools that solve problems I actually have.

Outside of client work, I run a homelab on an Intel N100 mini PC — Nextcloud, n8n automations, and a handful
of Dockerized side projects, all reachable only over a Tailscale mesh with no open ports. Running my own
infrastructure is where I learned what "production" really costs: backups, monitoring, and being the only
person who gets paged when something breaks at 3am.

I'm currently open to full-stack roles, especially ones that involve AI-assisted products, retrieval systems,
or infrastructure work.
